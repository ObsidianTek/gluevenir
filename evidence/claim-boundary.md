# Claim boundary

Status: active public claim boundary for the verified pre-release prototype.
Individual implementation, deployment, and benchmark statements still require
their corresponding machine-readable or live evidence.

- All demonstration data is synthetic, and automated detection is imperfect.
- Describe Gluevenir as AARM-inspired and mapped to evidence for supported
  memory-mediated actions only.
- Do not claim AARM conformance, certification, community verification,
  universal interception, or use an AARM logo without authorization.
- Do not claim AIUC-1 readiness, conformance, certification, assessment, or
  audit readiness. The prototype may show evidence relevant to selected
  controls only.
- Do not claim HIPAA compliance, clinical validation, legal advice, MNPI
  determinations, or guaranteed PII/PHI/IP/MNPI detection.
- Describe Recall Receipts as signed, content-bound, and independently
  verifiable within the documented development-key trust model. Do not call
  them immutable, tamper-proof, non-repudiable, or cryptographically attested.
- A sponsor tool, deployment, test, benchmark, or integration is verified only
  after the exact check succeeds and its redacted evidence is captured.
